## English (en-US)

**App name:** Snatchy: Lift Technique

**Short description:** Analyse your snatch and clean on your phone: phases, angles and progress.

**Full description:**

Snatchy turns a video of your lift into a clear, frame-by-frame breakdown — on your own phone, with no account and no upload.

Record or import a clip of a snatch or clean. Snatchy tracks your body and hands, finds each repetition and walks you through the movement:

• Movement phases — setup, first pull, transition, second pull, turnover, catch and recovery, each one tappable to jump to that frame.
• Measured checks — arms through the pull, hip and knee extension, receiving position and standing recovery, with the measured angle next to the target.
• Skeleton overlay and bar path — see the tracked joints, hands and estimated bar path on your video.
• Progress over time — a score trend for each movement, so you can see whether your technique is improving.
• Saved skeleton replay — reopen any saved lift and replay its tracked skeleton, even without the original video.

Supports Snatch, Power Snatch, Hang and High-Hang Snatch, Clean, Power Clean, Hang and High-Hang (Power) Clean and Muscle Clean, detected automatically or chosen manually.

Private by design: the analysis runs entirely on your device. Your videos and pose data never leave your phone. Optional, non-personalised ads support the app and can be turned off in Settings.

For the best results, film side-on at hip height with the whole body and both ends of the bar in frame.

Snatchy's scores are experimental estimates from pose-tracking models. They are not a substitute for a qualified coach, and you train at your own risk.

Snatchy transforme la vidéo de votre mouvement en une analyse claire, image par image, directement sur votre téléphone, sans compte et sans envoi.

Filmez ou importez un arraché ou un épaulé. Snatchy suit votre corps et vos mains, repère chaque répétition et vous guide à travers le mouvement :
