Try Snatchy before everyone else

Film your snatch or clean: Snatchy tracks your body and hands and breaks down every phase of the lift, right on your phone. Free, no account, and your videos never leave your phone.

  1. 1 Join the testers group

    Using the Google account on your Android phone, tap “Join group”.
    Join the group

  2. 2 Accept the test invitation

    Tap “Become a tester”.
    Become a tester

  3. 3 Install the app from Google Play

    If Play can't find it yet, wait a few minutes.
    Open Google Play

  4. 4 Keep it for 14 days and tell us what you think

    Analyse a few lifts and share feedback in the group. Google asks testers to stay enrolled for 14 days before the public release — thank you!
