Privacy policy

Effective 17 October 2026 · Snatchy for Android and web

In short

Snatchy analyses your lifts on your own device. Your videos and the body-pose data measured from them are never uploaded by Snatchy, and there is no account or Snatchy server. The only third party is Google AdMob, which shows optional ads and is described below.

What Snatchy processes on your device

  • Videos you record or import. They are read on the device to measure body and hand positions. Snatchy does not copy or store the video file; it is available only while the app is open.
  • Pose data. Body, hand and 3D joint positions for each analysed frame, plus the detected bar position. They are stored on the device so you can replay a saved lift as a skeleton and re-analyse it. They contain no images.
  • Analysis summaries. Exercise, phases, measured checks, scores and the hand/bar path of up to ten recent lifts, used for your history and progress chart.
  • Preferences. Language, theme and whether optional ads are enabled.

All of this stays in the app's private storage on your device (browser or WebView local storage and IndexedDB). Snatchy does not sell, share or transfer it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When optional ads are enabled, the Android app shows a banner served by Google AdMob. Snatchy requests non-personalised ads only. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and ad interactions. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can turn ads off entirely under Settings → Optional advertising; coaching suggestions are then disabled, while measurements and scores remain.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Snatchy never passes your videos, pose data or results to Google or any other party.

Permissions

Snatchy uses internet access only to load ads. Recording a video opens your device's camera app; importing opens the system file picker, which gives Snatchy access only to the file you choose.

Deleting your data

Remove a lift from History to delete its summary and saved pose data. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes everything Snatchy stored. Because Snatchy has no server, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Snatchy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

Not medical or coaching advice

Snatchy's phases, checks and scores are experimental estimates from 2D and 3D pose models. They can be wrong, are not a substitute for a qualified coach or medical professional, and you train at your own risk.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date. Questions or deletion requests: github.com/FedeLoch/Snatchy/issues.
